The pause doesn’t appear

The pause is started by a Shortcuts automation. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation and check that the automation for the app exists and is set to run immediately. In 2min you can add it again from Settings → Social apps.

Screen time statistics are empty

2min needs Screen Time access. Open 2min, choose your apps in Settings → Social apps, and allow access when iOS asks. The system updates statistics with a short delay.

The time limit closes an app

When the time you chose is up, iOS shows the “Time’s up” screen for that app only. Other apps keep working. You can turn the limit off in 2min Settings.

Restore or cancel a subscription

To restore purchases, open the Pro screen in 2min and tap “Restore purchases”. To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 2min.
